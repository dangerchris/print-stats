General

Machine time covers wear and depreciation; leave at 0 to count only filament and electricity.

Time-of-use electricity

Each power reading is charged at the rate in force when it was taken. Weekends are off-peak all day, and so are holidays if a holiday list is selected. Weekday hours are 24-hour ranges with the end hour excluded, e.g. 7-11,17-19; any hour not listed is off-peak. When this is off, the flat rate above applies.

Energy estimate

Used until a power meter is connected. Each sample's draw is the base load plus each heater's full-power watts times its duty cycle. The defaults fit the base K2 on 110 V (rated 450 W; the bed runs at half of its 700 W element).

Override the material price for a specific spool. An override applies only while that colour is loaded in the slot.
